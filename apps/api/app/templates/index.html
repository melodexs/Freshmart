Questions, answered

Where do you deliver?

We deliver in {{ areas|map(attribute="name")|join(", ") }}. Each area has its own delivery fee, which you see before you place an order.

How can I pay?

Pay the rider when your order arrives{% if bank.ready %}, or pay ahead by bank transfer. A pay-ahead order is packed once we confirm your payment{% endif %}. We never ask for card details on this site.

How do I track my order?

Tap Track, then enter your order reference and the phone number you used. You will see where your order is.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, from My Orders, until it leaves the store.

What if an item is out of stock?

It is marked out of stock and cannot be added to your cart. Items with low stock show how many are left.
